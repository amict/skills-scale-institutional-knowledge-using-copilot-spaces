# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts connect business needs to implementation decisions. They clarify requirements, validate outcomes, and help the team translate stakeholder intent into actionable work.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements and workflows
- Define acceptance criteria and success measures with stakeholders
- Analyze process gaps, dependencies, and opportunities for improvement
- Validate solution approaches against business goals and constraints
- Support prioritization conversations with Product Managers and Project Managers

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to refine scope and success metrics
- Partners with Developers to ensure the implementation matches the intended user and business outcome
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align requirements, milestones, and stakeholder communications
- Brings stakeholder feedback into backlog refinement and release readiness discussions

### Typical engagement
- Discovery and planning phases
- Backlog refinement and sprint readiness reviews
- Validation before release and during change-impact assessments

### Decision-making authority
- Has decision-making authority for requirement clarity and business process interpretation
- Escalates unresolved trade-offs or conflicting stakeholder priorities to the Product Manager or Project Sponsor

---

## Compliance/Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance and Risk Officers protect the organization by ensuring projects meet regulatory, policy, and risk requirements before work is committed or released.

### Responsibilities
- Assess project risks, controls, and regulatory obligations
- Review requirements for data privacy, security, compliance, and operational risk
- Validate that releases meet policy and governance standards
- Escalate high-risk issues and approve mitigation plans when needed
- Support audit and reporting activities throughout the lifecycle

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Advises Project Managers on risk tracking, mitigation planning, and escalation paths
- Collaborates with Developers and Technical Architects to review security and implementation controls
- Works with Product Managers to ensure roadmap priorities consider compliance constraints
- Helps Release Managers confirm that deployment readiness aligns with policy and operational standards

### Typical engagement
- Planning and risk review stages
- Major milestone approvals
- Pre-release and post-release compliance checks

### Decision-making authority
- Has authority to approve or reject work that does not meet policy or compliance requirements
- Escalates unresolved compliance concerns to leadership or governance channels when necessary

---

## End-User Representative

### Role Summary
End-User Representatives ensure project decisions remain grounded in actual user needs, usability, and adoption outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Share real-world user needs, pain points, and workflow expectations
- Validate requirements for usability, accessibility, and business fit
- Provide feedback on prototypes, workflows, and release readiness
- Represent the voice of the user during prioritization and quality reviews

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Provides input to Product Managers and Business Analysts during discovery and prioritization
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to validate usability and acceptance criteria
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing adoption risks and stakeholder concerns early
- Works with Release Managers to clarify communication needs for rollout and change support

### Typical engagement
- Requirements definition and user validation
- User acceptance testing and feedback collection
- Release planning and adoption readiness reviews

### Decision-making authority
- Makes recommendations based on user impact and adoption quality
- Escalates unresolved product or usability concerns to Product Managers or stakeholders for prioritization

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the planning, readiness, and execution of software deployment activities so updates are predictable, well-communicated, and operationally safe.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules, dependencies, and deployment windows
- Validate release readiness across engineering, QA, and operations
- Communicate deployment status and rollout plans to stakeholders
- Manage rollback readiness and issue triage during release execution
- Track outcomes and lessons learned after deployment

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to align release timing with project milestones
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to confirm build quality and test completion
- Partners with Compliance/Risk Officers to verify policy and operational checks before rollout
- Supports Product Managers and stakeholders with communication around scope, timing, and impact

### Typical engagement
- Pre-release planning
- Deployment execution
- Post-release monitoring and follow-up

### Decision-making authority
- Has authority to decide release timing and readiness within approved release windows
- Escalates deployment risks or blockers to Project Managers and leadership when mitigation is required

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical direction and design oversight so solutions remain scalable, secure, resilient, and aligned with the target platform.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and design for feasibility, scalability, and maintainability
- Define technical standards, patterns, and nonfunctional requirements
- Identify major integration, performance, and security risks early
- Support design trade-off decisions during planning and implementation
- Guide Developers and delivery teams on platform and system constraints

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers and Business Analysts on technical feasibility and implementation trade-offs
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure the solution design matches quality and performance standards
- Partners with Project Managers to assess dependencies, risk, and milestone impacts
- Works with Compliance/Risk Officers to confirm secure and compliant design choices

### Typical engagement
- Planning and architectural review sessions
- High-impact design decisions and integration strategy
- Major change assessments and technical risk review

### Decision-making authority
- Has authority to recommend or require architectural standards and technical constraints
- Escalates unresolved design risks or platform conflicts to engineering leadership for resolution
